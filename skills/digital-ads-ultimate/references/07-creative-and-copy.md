# 07 Creative, Copy, and Compliance

Creative is the largest lever on Meta and TikTok, a major one on LinkedIn and Demand Gen, and the Quality Score driver on Search. Build a system, not one-off ads.

## 1. Research inputs (gather before writing)

- Voice of customer: reviews (yours and competitors'), WhatsApp/DM questions, sales call notes, support tickets, comments, forums, marketplace reviews. Capture exact phrases.
- Competitor ads: Meta Ad Library, TikTok Creative Center, LinkedIn Ad Library, Google Ads Transparency Center. Extract angles and formats, not wording.
- Search data: keywords and "People also ask" for questions and objections.
- Performance data: top creatives by thumb-stop, hold, CTR, CPA from past accounts.

Output a one-page **messaging map**: audience segment, core pain, desired outcome, objections, proof available, offer, and the single promise per concept.

## 2. Awareness stages and what each ad does

| Stage | Buyer thinks | Ad job | Proof needed |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Unaware | Not seeing a problem | Surface the problem through a relatable moment | Story, stat |
| Problem-aware | "I have this issue" | Name it precisely, promise a path | Empathy, explanation |
| Solution-aware | "There are ways to fix it" | Position your approach over alternatives | Comparison, mechanism |
| Product-aware | "I know you exist" | Remove doubts | Reviews, demos, guarantees you honor |
| Most-aware | "Ready to act" | Make acting easy | Offer, deadline that is real, simple CTA |

Prospecting speaks mostly to the first three. Retargeting speaks to the last two.

## 3. Angle library (pick one per concept)

| Angle | Pattern |
| --- | --- |
| Pain removal | "[Pain] costs you [time/money]. Here is a way to stop it." |
| Outcome with timeframe | "[Result] in [period], without [common sacrifice]." (only if true and typical) |
| Myth-busting | "You were told [belief]. The data says [fact]." |
| Comparison | "[Our way] vs [usual way], side by side." |
| How it works | Show the mechanism, step by step |
| Mistake/warning | "The [N] mistakes that make [outcome] fail." |
| Social proof | A named, real customer story with specifics |
| Founder/expert POV | Why this exists, what the expert saw |
| Price/value | Cost per use, total cost vs alternative |
| Identity | "For [specific type of person] who [specific situation]." |
| Seasonal/event | Tied to a real calendar moment |
| Risk reversal | Real trial, return, refund, or free consultation terms |

## 4. Copy frameworks

| Framework | Use for | Skeleton |
| --- | --- | --- |
| PAS | Pain-led cold ads | Problem -> Agitate (cost of inaction) -> Solution |
| AIDA | Short ads, landing hero | Attention -> Interest -> Desire -> Action |
| BAB | Transformations | Before -> After -> Bridge |
| 4U | Headlines | Useful, Urgent, Unique, Ultra-specific |
| FAB | Product features | Feature -> Advantage -> Benefit |
| PASTOR | Long-form/sales pages | Problem, Amplify, Story, Testimony, Offer, Response |
| Hook-Proof-Offer | Social video scripts | Hook line -> evidence/demo -> clear offer + CTA |

## 5. Hook bank (fill with real facts)

```
"Stop [common action] if you want [desired result]."
"[Number] [people/customers] switched from [alternative]. Here is why."
"I spent [amount/time] on [problem] so you do not have to."
"What [role] wishes they knew before [decision]."
"This is what [specific number] looks like in [timeframe] for [specific persona]."
"If [specific situation], read this before you [action]."
"Why does [surprising observation]?"
"[Competitor type] charge [amount]. We charge [amount] because [reason]."
"Wait, you are still [doing X] manually?"
"Three signs your [asset] is costing you [metric]."
```

Indonesian versions should read like a person talking, not a translation. Prefer "Kamu" or "Anda" consistently with brand voice, keep local idioms natural, and avoid stiff openers.

## 6. Platform copy specs (verify current limits)

| Platform | Elements | Typical limits and notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Google RSA | Headlines/descriptions | Up to 15 headlines x 30 chars, 4 descriptions x 90 chars; path fields 15 chars each |
| Google PMax/Demand Gen | Headlines, long headlines, descriptions | Short headlines ~30 chars, long headline ~90 chars, descriptions up to ~90 chars; check asset specs |
| Meta | Primary text, headline, description | Headline kept short (~40 chars), primary text first line carries the hook; visible text truncates on mobile |
| LinkedIn | Intro text, headline | Intro truncates around 150 chars on feed, headline ~70 chars |
| TikTok | Ad text | Short (up to ~100 chars), the video is the copy; on-screen text and voiceover do the persuading |

## 7. Landing-to-ad message match

Same promise, same words, same visual cue. If the ad says "Free 20 minute audit", the page headline says it and the form is for it.

## 9. Production loop

1. Weekly: choose 2 to 4 concepts from the messaging map.
2. Produce 2 to 3 hook variants each (change only the first seconds or first line).
3. Launch in test cells with equal budget (or in the shared test campaign).
4. Judge after fair spend (see `11`). Promote winners, archive losers with notes.
5. Iterate on winners: change one element (hook, thumbnail, CTA, length, talent).
6. Log everything in a creative library with IDs matching UTM `utm_content`.

## 10. Quality bar for copy

Checks to run before finalizing any ad or page:

1. **Clarity**: a stranger understands what it is and who it is for in one read.
2. **Specificity**: numbers, mechanisms, and named outcomes, not adjectives.
3. **So what**: each claim answers "why should I care".
4. **Proof**: each claim has support the business can show.
5. **Voice**: consistent register, like a person talking.
6. **Friction**: the CTA names the exact action and what happens next.
7. **Risk**: truthful risk reducers only.
8. **Banned patterns**: no em dashes, no empty superlatives ("best", "number one", "revolutionary"), no invented numbers, no stacked adjective triplets, no symmetric slogan filler.
9. **Emoji**: follow brand voice. When uncertain, use none.

## 11. Claims and compliance

General:
- No guaranteed or typical-results promises you cannot substantiate. Do not imply income, health, or financial outcomes.
- Testimonials must be real, consented, and representative. Disclose material connections and incentives. Label sponsored content as required.
- Comparative claims need evidence. Do not misuse competitor trademarks in copy.
- Pricing, discounts, deadlines, stock limits must be real and visible on the landing page.
- Do not target or imply knowledge of sensitive personal traits.
- Honor platform restricted-category lists (health, finance, crypto, gambling, alcohol, political, adult, weapons, children).

Indonesia (not legal advice, confirm with counsel):
- Etika Pariwara Indonesia (advertising ethics code) applies to ad conduct.
- OJK rules for financial products and investment promotion, including risk disclosure.
- BPOM requirements for food, health, supplement, and cosmetic claims and registration numbers.
- UU PDP (Law 27/2022) for personal data collection and use.
- Consumer protection law (UU Perlindungan Konsumen) on misleading information.
- Halal labeling rules where applicable.

Process: scan every ad for policy-risk words before upload, keep an evidence file for claims, and when rejected fix the cause rather than resubmitting repeatedly.

## 12. Creative testing hygiene

- Test big differences (concept, hook, format) before small ones (button color).
- Hold audience, budget, and placement constant across variants in a test cell.
- Run to a pre-set rule (see `11`), not until you like the result.
- Fatigue is a rate: track CTR, hook rate, and CPA trend per creative week over week with frequency.

# 08 Landing Page and Funnel

Paid traffic is rented attention. Conversion rate decides whether you can afford it. A 1 point lift in landing conversion has the same effect on CPA as a 20 to 50% drop in CPC when the base rate is low.

## 1. Fast diagnostic

| Observation | Probable layer |
| --- | --- |
| High CTR, low landing conversion | Message mismatch, slow page, weak offer, friction |
| High bounce on mobile, fine on desktop | Mobile UX, speed, layout |
| Add-to-cart ok, checkout drop | Shipping cost surprise, payment methods, trust, form errors |
| Leads many, qualified few | Offer attracts wrong people, no qualification, wrong platform event |
| Form starts high, submits low | Too many fields, validation errors, privacy fear |
| Good page, bad follow-up | Slow response, no sales process |

## 2. Page structure for paid traffic

1. **Hero**: headline states the promise for the clicking segment, a subhead with mechanism or proof, one primary CTA, one supporting visual showing the product or outcome.
2. **Proof strip**: real logos, review count and rating, press, or a quantified result with source.
3. **Problem/benefit block**: 3 to 5 concrete benefits written in the customer's words.
4. **How it works**: 3 steps.
5. **Offer**: what is included, price, bonuses, terms.
6. **Detailed proof**: testimonials with names/faces/context, case studies, demos.
7. **Objections**: FAQ addressing price, time, risk, fit, delivery, refund.
8. **Final CTA**: repeats the hero CTA with a short reason.
9. **Footer**: privacy, terms, contact (required for trust and ad policy).

One page, one goal. Remove top navigation for dedicated landing pages. Keep one primary CTA repeated.

## 3. Speed and mobile

- Most paid social and TikTok traffic is mobile, inside in-app browsers. Test there.
- Target a fast first view: compress images, serve modern formats, lazy-load below the fold, minimize third-party scripts, avoid layout shift. Core Web Vitals guidance: LCP under about 2.5 s, INP under about 200 ms, CLS under about 0.1 (Google's published "good" thresholds).
- Tap targets large, forms with the right input types (tel, email), autofill, sticky CTA on mobile.
- No intrusive interstitials right after the click.

## 4. Forms and lead capture

- Fewer fields convert more but may lower quality. Start with name, WhatsApp/phone or email, and one qualifying question. Add fields when volume is high and quality needs filtering.
- Show what happens next ("We reply on WhatsApp within 15 minutes, 09:00 to 18:00 WIB").
- Inline validation and clear error messages. Never wipe the form on error.
- Confirm submission on a thank-you page that fires the conversion event once. Use the thank-you page to book a call, join WhatsApp, or share a resource.
- Consent checkbox and privacy link where required.

## 5. Ecommerce checkout friction

- Show total cost early (shipping, tax, fees). Surprise costs are a top abandonment cause.
- Offer payment methods local buyers use: bank transfer/VA, e-wallets (GoPay, OVO, DANA, ShopeePay), QRIS, credit/debit, COD where appropriate, installment where relevant.
- Guest checkout, address autofill, clear delivery estimate.
- Trust: returns policy, secure payment cues, contact info, real reviews.
- Cart recovery: retargeting ads plus WhatsApp/email reminders (with consent).

## 6. Offer and pricing tests (highest leverage)

Test before cosmetics: price anchoring, bundles, bonuses, free shipping threshold, trial length, payment plans, guarantee terms. Test one at a time, with traffic enough to read the result (see `11`).

## 7. Message match checklist

- Headline repeats the ad's promise.
- Imagery or video continues the ad's scene.
- Offer, price, and deadline match the ad exactly.
- The CTA button uses the same verb as the ad.
- For search, the keyword theme appears in the headline.

## 8. Lead handling (where most lead-gen money leaks)

| Item | Standard |
| --- | --- |
| Response time | Under 5 minutes in business hours; automated instant acknowledgement outside hours |
| Channel | WhatsApp or phone first for local/B2C; email + call for B2B |
| Follow-up | Defined cadence (for example day 0, 1, 3, 7) |
| Qualification | A short script or form fields with a score |
| Feedback loop | CRM stage updates sent back to ad platforms |
| Owner | Named person and backup |

Measure speed to lead, contact rate, qualified rate, and close rate per campaign. Feed these into the CPL target.

## 9. CRO test queue (typical order)

1. Message match and hero clarity.
2. Offer and price presentation.
3. Social proof placement and quality.
4. Form length/fields and CTA wording.
5. Page speed and mobile layout fixes.
6. Page length and section order.
7. Trust elements (guarantees, security, support).
8. Visuals (product shots, video).

## 10. Measuring page performance

- Landing page conversion rate = conversions / landing page sessions (not clicks, since clicks include bots and bounces before load). Compare click-to-landing-page-view ratio to detect slow load or tracking loss.
- Segment by source, device, campaign, creative.
- Use session recordings and heatmaps to find friction. Survey buyers: what almost stopped you?
